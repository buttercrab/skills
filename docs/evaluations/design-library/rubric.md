# Independent judgment rubric

Status: pre-run proposal; no results. Apply only after protocol and corpus/key freeze. Private role keys live beside each prompt; actual-card mappings are independently produced after catalog freeze. A mapping is an acceptable set, not a list of mandatory favorites.

## Gates before scores

- Record condition isolation, same-corpus/source/tool availability, coordinator fidelity and held-out exposure. A broken pair is not a controlled comparison.
- Record permission/scope compliance. A policy workaround invalidates affected acceptance; preserve outputs without promoting them to passes.
- Record evidence integrity: fabricated source, invented inspection or misleading current/visual/interaction claim is a material defect regardless of polished output. Mark claim and affected dimensions; do not bury it in a mean score.
- If an outcome could not be observed, use **unassessed**. If a role is unnecessary (notably broad retrieval in case 06), use **N/A**. Absence of rendered work is expected, not a defect.

## Dimensions (independent 0–3 ordinal judgments)

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Context and proportional initiative | Overrides key context or delegation | Major unknowns missed or gratuitous interview/tour | Material context handled, minor inefficiency | Consequential unknowns resolved/marked; sufficient brief, delegation and narrow scope honored |
| Discovery coverage | No relevant role found; forces a recommendation despite gap | Familiar/shallow region only; misses decisive roles | Useful required roles found with some meaningful alternative/gap coverage | Relevant role families and contextual alternatives found efficiently; misses and external leads honestly scoped |
| Contextual relevance and mismatch | Consequential selected mismatch ignored | Fit based mainly on labels/appearance; weak adaptation | Most selected roles useful with explicit mismatches | Each consequential transfer fits behavior/audience; attractive mismatch and structural match handled deliberately |
| Evidence discipline | Fabricates/overclaims source or observation | Receipts too vague or modalities conflated | Traceable material support and honest limits with minor gaps | Precise claim-specific locators/versions/access; independent card/primary/visual/flow/test boundaries preserved |
| Human direction and revision | Rejects/overwrites explicit direction | Acknowledges answer but artifact barely changes | Direction materially changes selection/application | Keep/reject/mix/delegation/explore-only instruction consistently controls revised packet and outline |
| Substantive application | No useful outline or contradicts required lifecycle | Generic inspiration and token citations | Concrete correct outline with some source-to-decision links | Specific source portions produce coherent states/content/recovery and context-specific adaptation; alternatives or gaps handled concretely |
| Output usefulness and scope | Unusable or wrong deliverable | Hard to act on, oversized or materially incomplete | Reviewable packet/outline with limited omissions | Compact actionable comparison plus applied outline, assumptions and unresolved checks, within requested scope |

Do not score question number, aesthetic preference, resource brand, framework choice, card quantity or prose length. Contextually justified alternative solutions can earn full marks. A resource selection can be useful while its visual fit remains unassessed. Visual evidence honesty and visual attractiveness are different questions.

## Relevance accounting

The independent mapper records for each case:

- Required problem roles, optional supporting roles and roles that may honestly remain unmatched.
- Applicable card/source IDs by role, inspectable portions and limits; known wrong-context decoys with the specific mismatch, not a blanket brand exclusion.
- Complete-match availability and whether further external inspection could resolve a gap.

Compute role coverage as roles materially addressed / applicable declared roles, with denominator and N/A shown. Report retrieval coverage (candidate considered) separately from selection coverage (supported candidate chosen) and application coverage (decision present in outline). A runner need not select every admissible card: interchangeable cards cover the same role. For case 06, a correct facts-based repair can receive full marks without external retrieval. For case 08, complete-match recall is N/A if none exists; score credible limited transfers and honest gap handling.

For each selected consequential recommendation, judge **fit / qualified transfer / unsupported / mismatch**. Report counts and the concrete effect; do not assign a favorable precision ratio to a source list that never affects the output. Rejected candidates can demonstrate mismatch understanding without becoming selection failures. A visual-only reference can validly contribute atmosphere while a different reference supplies behavior.

## Application audit

Trace a small set of material decisions from requirement or delivered fact → inspected resource portion if used → borrow/adapt/reject decision → exact outline section. Credit supplied product facts directly where sufficient. Merely citing/opening a resource earns no application credit. Identify contradictions such as “pending” copy paired with a confirmed reservation, or undo promised after an irreversible transition. Verify the specific state/content outline against the case key; do not invent additional product requirements.

## Reviewer record

For each dimension save rating, concise evidence locator, material defect or uncertainty and confidence. Keep source-card, primary-source, visual-view, documented-flow and tested-interaction status separate. Say “not observed” when appropriate. Interaction, rendered accessibility, native layout and preference satisfaction are outside this cohort.

A final report contains paired per-case judgments and salient tradeoffs, not an unqualified aggregate score. Any aggregate is descriptive with exclusions and denominators, and cannot convert small sample observations into effectiveness/reliability claims. Scripted user fidelity does not establish real-user satisfaction. No trials, source views or visual inspections may be inferred from this fixture author's documents.
