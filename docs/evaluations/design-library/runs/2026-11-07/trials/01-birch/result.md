## First-visit flow

1. **Scan QR → Welcome / what happens.** Understand the exchange from a short explanation and one concrete scene. See the next meeting summary immediately below it, without a menu or account gate.
2. **Follow “Next meeting details” → Meeting / arrival section.** See date, time, venue, free admission, step-free entrance and how to arrive alone. This is a same-page anchor on the proposed short site, so Back does not restart a funnel.
3. **Choose how to attend.** “You can just come along” is a complete outcome. “Reserve a chair (optional)” opens a short reservation screen only if desired.
4. **Optional reservation → explicit result.** Confirm a chair only after success; preserve entered information after a correctable error. Return to meeting details without losing context.

People who stop after reading should still have the information needed to attend. No “Join Lantern Table” step belongs in this first visit.

Avoid a large decorative hero that pushes the date far down a phone screen. No recruitment badges, member counters, teacher rankings or ornamental motion.

Use real heading structure, descriptive links, visible keyboard focus and accessible labels; contrast, touch targets and zoom/reflow need checking in a later build.
