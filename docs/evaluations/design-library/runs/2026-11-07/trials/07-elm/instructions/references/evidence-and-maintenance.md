# Evidence and maintenance

This pilot translates selected evidence into task guidance. The instructions are proposals whose usefulness must be checked in actual work. Standards, practitioner experience and experimental results have different scopes; no single source validates the entire workflow.

## Selected foundations

- [Horvitz, mixed-initiative interfaces](https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf), CHI 1999, principles 5–11: weighs key uncertainty against interruption cost and preserves direct control, refinement and interaction history. A design argument, not proof of an optimal question policy.
- [Nuseibeh and Easterbrook, requirements engineering](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~sme/papers/2000/ICSE2000.pdf), sections 4.3 and 8: select elicitation techniques for the context and support evolving requirements. A research roadmap, not a mandatory interview sequence.
- [Tohidi and colleagues, multiple designs](https://www.billbuxton.com/rightDesign.pdf), CHI 2006: alternatives encouraged critical feedback in a 48-person thermostat paper-prototype study; substantial redesign suggestions did not significantly increase. Options need interpretation and follow-through, not an assumption that selection alone produces creativity.
- [Dow and colleagues, parallel prototyping](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2010/parallel-prototyping/ParallelPrototyping2010-final.pdf), 2010: reports benefits in a bounded advertisement-design task with nonprofessionals. It does not justify making several complete products for every request.
- [Zhang and Choi, clarification](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-naacl.306.pdf), 2025: models clarification under fixed budgets with generated oracle question/answer pairs. It does not measure the effort of real people directing creative work.
- [GOV.UK form structure](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/form-structure): question purpose, one-thing defaults and repeated-task exceptions. Public-service guidance, not a universal screen-count rule.
- [GOV.UK check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/): correction routes and preserved answers. Placement depends on transaction context.
- [Figma's UI3 iteration account](https://www.figma.com/blog/our-approach-to-designing-ui3/): changes to hidden state, direct controls and panel behavior. First-party reasoning with undisclosed study methods, not a general effect estimate.
- [Carbon empty states](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/patterns/empty-states): cause, available action and space change the treatment. Design-system practice.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/), [text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html), [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), [focus appearance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html): distinguish normative requirements, conformance level, explanatory guidance and exceptions.
- [ARIA APG introduction](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/practices/read-me-first/): semantics do not automatically supply keyboard behavior; examples need relevant browser/assistive-technology checks.
- [Atlassian typography](https://atlassian.design/foundations/typography): an explicit contextual product/brand system, illustrating why house fonts and heading weights are not universal fundamentals.

Sources were inspected on 2026-11-07. Core rules preserve the person's stated intent, existing answers, delegation and ability to reject suggested alternatives. They do not mandate a fixed interview or recurring approvals. An agent can contribute concrete possibilities while the person controls the desired direction.

## Revise from evidence

Refresh exact standard/tool claims when their versions change. For question-asking, inspect missed consequential uncertainty, repeated questions, leading alternatives, ignored answers, needless approvals and failure to execute after direction is supplied. Distinguish a bad instruction from missing product facts, unavailable tools or a flawed evaluator.

Use task outcomes and critique to narrow or remove rules. A user preference or project convention can guide that work without becoming a universal aesthetic ban. Keep the detailed source record and trial artifacts outside the active instruction body; load only what the current decision needs.
