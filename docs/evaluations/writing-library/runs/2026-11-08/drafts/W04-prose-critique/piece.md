# Your first visit to Lantern Room

Bring photo ID and a document showing a local address if you want to join Lantern Room. Membership is free for adults aged 18 or over, and you join in person. The room lends household tools and board games from beside the Elm Street bus stop.

Choose a visit during opening hours: Wednesdays from 17:00 to 19:00 or Saturdays from 10:00 to 13:00. These are also the times when you can return borrowed items, so check that a return visit will fit your plans before you take something home.

For joining, the requirement is a local address, but the information available does not specify the boundary or which documents staff accept. If you are unsure whether your address qualifies or what document to bring, ask staff to clarify those details. A photo ID and an address document are both required.

You can look at the online list to see the types of items Lantern Room lends. Treat it as a guide to what the room offers, rather than a check of what is on the shelf that day. It does not show live availability. Items cannot be reserved, so a listing is no guarantee that a particular tool or game will be there when you arrive.

Each member may borrow up to two items at once, for seven days. If you have a question about how an item works, staff can explain it at the desk. Ask before taking it home if you need that explanation. Lantern Room is a lending room, not a workshop or a repair service.

Return your items during opening hours. There is no drop box, so you cannot leave a return outside those times. The seven-day loan and the opening schedule are worth checking together when you plan your borrowing.

If an item stops working, tell staff when you return it. Do not attempt electrical repairs yourself; bring the problem to staff's attention at the return desk.
