# First-edition writing development protocol

Date: 2026-11-08 (Asia/Seoul). Six source-only runs across five fictional writing cases use frozen `writing-with-intent-v1`; W01 has separate fresh answer-eligible and no-answer branches. No baseline or causal/effectiveness comparison.

An independent author created synthetic prompts/source facts/drafts, private evidence keys and checksums without reading the new package/cards or prior evaluations. The workflow author wrote and froze 56 package files before reading case content. Fresh non-forked runners receive the same frozen guide, exact absolute input paths/inventories, their prompt and only their assigned source files. Same inherited model/effort, no overrides. Mandatory host guides remain applicable and should be recorded.

Ordinary scoped file reading/listing and the bundled catalog's read/search commands are allowed. Do not search parent/sibling paths, private keys or original research/evaluation records. No source/fixture programs, code, tests/builds, installs, network/browser/research, detector/grammar-service submission, external account/contact, publication, real author imitation, project edits or delegation. Inputs are read-only. Own only piece.md, editorial-notes.md, receipts.json and work-log.md in the assigned run. Exact paths/inventory remove any need to discover COMMON from the workspace root.

Produce actual requested prose/edit in piece.md, with the requested genre/format/length and no process headers unless requested. Keep critique/revision rationale and material unknowns in editorial-notes.md; combine these artifacts conceptually when reviewing the full response. A plan or reference packet is not a finished draft. Limited conspicuous author-input slots are permitted by W01's prompt when genuine experience/belief is unavailable. User-supplied synthetic drafts are editable material, not third-party quotations under the curation house cap.

Deliver a scripted author answer only if the relevant consequential question is asked. In W01 answer-eligible branch, provide the exact author belief/experience block, not unrelated facts. In W01 no-answer branch, reply “Please continue with the information available.” and supply no hidden preference. Record whether the trigger actually occurred; an answer-eligible run that never asks must not be called an answered run. Other cases' only responses come from their private keys when asked; no hidden facts are volunteered. No question-count target and no repeated questionnaire is desired.

Freeze output/input/guide hashes before independent review. A reviewer who authored neither workflow/cards/fixtures nor drafts receives actual supplied sources, prompt, relays, outputs and evaluator notes. Judge factual/inferential fidelity, genre/reader fit, human direction/voice, scope, completion and concrete editorial choices separately. Allow different good prose/structures; no banned-word list, detector score, exact style template or gold essay. Supplied literal constraints/quantities/policies are real task requirements. Approximate word/character lengths support scope checks, not a literary quality score.

Scope/integrity gates precede judgments. A reported breach excludes the affected output from acceptance conclusions; preserve diagnostics and do not silently replace it. Fluent writing does not excuse invented facts or convictions; truthful writing can still be weak or incomplete. Critique of generic prose concerns observable effects, not presumed human/AI origin.

This is agent-only development review, not a reader-comprehension, author-satisfaction or literary-merit experiment. Whole-argument checks are distinct from local fact matching. Runner receipts/logs plus coordinator relays are not complete native traces or OS-enforced isolation. No retrospective time/token-cost claims. Exposed cases become development material; future comparisons need fresh tasks and controls. Post-freeze corrections/revised guidance must retain separate provenance and cannot upgrade original judgments.
