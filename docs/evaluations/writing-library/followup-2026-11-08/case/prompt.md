I haven't settled the personal point, and I haven't supplied a personal incident that would earn it. Please don't choose my belief for me or give me a satisfying experience I didn't report.

I don't need a grand lesson or a familiar volunteer-appreciation speech.

Use your judgment about the shape, opening, and ending. The piece can be plain, lightly funny, reflective, practical, or a mixture, as long as it sounds like one person addressing people they know. Please work only from the supplied notes.
