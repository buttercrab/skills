# Evaluator private
## Targeted answer
A question about what submission commits to, review/approval, stock allocation or the request lifecycle unlocks: "Submission only joins a review queue. A studio manager approves or declines it after checking stock. Nothing is reserved until approval. For the local demo, let me simulate approve and decline for each pending request. Declined requests should let the person revise the quantity and resubmit for review. Quantity must be a whole number from 1 to 20."
If asked purely style questions, reply: "The important unknown is what submitting actually commits us to. Please establish that first." The explicit prompt requires establishing lifecycle before choosing final status/confirmation. A bland 'submitted' default with no inquiry misses it.
## Observable outcomes
Question targets the consequential lifecycle unknown before finalizing its meaning. Final page clearly communicates queued review and no reservation until approval. Creating valid request yields pending review, not approved/guaranteed. Demo approve and decline act on an individual request, yielding distinct visible states. Decline offers revision and resubmission into pending review; demonstrate at least one decline/revise/resubmit and one approve. Empty, 0, 21 and fractional quantities cannot create a request and have useful correction guidance. Do not require a specific visual pattern or exact status words if their meaning is clear. Verify actual states in the rendered page.
