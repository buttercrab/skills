# Independent review: writing evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-07. Scope: six consequential empirical claims in `writing.md` and its source registry, checked against original methods/results/limitations rather than abstracts alone. This is a targeted cross-review, not a systematic replication or review of every source. Writer files were not edited by this reviewer.

## Findings

| Claim | Result | Evidence and required precision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doshi/Hauser: creativity ratings can improve while collective diversity falls. | Supported, with one wording correction communicated and applied. | [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00506v3) confirms v3, 2024-03-14. The [manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00506) describes 293 analyzed writers across three arms, not 293 treated writers; optional GPT-4 ideas appear only in treatment. Relevant locators: PDF pp.2–3, methods pp.12–13, similarity pp.9–10. Ratings and embedding similarity support different constructs; neither establishes professional long-form quality. |
| Noy/Zhang: the inspected early draft concerns 444 participants and bounded professional tasks. | Supported; an additional primary manuscript resolves part of the access gap. | [March 10 draft](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/Noy_Zhang_1_0.pdf), cover and abstract, explicitly says working paper/not peer reviewed and N444. The [author-hosted later manuscript](https://shakkednoy.com/Noy%20Zhang%20NBER%20SI.pdf) gives N453 (pp.1/3), GPT-3.5 (p.3), and excludes precise factual/context-specific demands (pp.6–7). The [author's publication list](https://shakkednoy.com/) links it under Science 381(6654). Keep versions separate; byte identity with the publisher PDF was not established. |
| Dell'Acqua et al.: higher recommendation ratings can coexist with incorrect strategic recommendations. | Supported; terminology and pagination should be narrowed. | [Working paper](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2023-10/SSRN-id4573321.pdf), §3.2, printed pp.13–15 and Table 6. The measure is human-rated Recommendation Quality, not established here as prose-only quality. Physical PDF pages are 17–19; web extraction P16–P18 is zero-based. This constructed outside-capability task does not estimate failure frequency in arbitrary business writing. |
| Liang et al.: detector outputs are unsuitable as writing-quality acceptance criteria. | Source findings supported; acceptance criterion remains a justified recommendation, not a measured intervention. | [Original v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02819v3), Results and Data: historical detectors, 91 TOEFL essays and 88 US school essays, plus rewriting sensitivity. Nonmatched populations and dated tools prevent a contemporary universal detector ranking. The dossier preserves those limits and does not recommend evasion. |
| Porter/Machery: sampled AI poems can receive higher ratings while human-origin framing improves ratings. | Supported with bounded scope already present. | [Study 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11564748/), results and methods: 696 participants assigned human/AI/unlabeled framing; GPT-3.5 poems versus selected established poets. Perceived originality is an exception to the broad quality advantage. Nonexpert preference is not expert literary merit or article-writing quality. |
| König et al.: little source-label devaluation in short nutrition texts contrasts with poetry framing effects. | Supported as a contextual contrast, not a direct contradictory replication. | [Methods/results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13641973/): AI versus no-source, three topics, 774 analyzed readers; most missed the label. No expert/human label arm. “No statistically significant effect” should not become proof of no effect. Awareness-based comparisons are post-treatment observations, not the randomized label effect. |

## Changes communicated

The writer corrected the Doshi treatment-denominator wording and independently opened the later Noy manuscript, adding it as a separately versioned source. The writer was also asked to use Recommendation Quality for Dell'Acqua and correct physical-PDF pagination. These are targeted clarifications; the evidence does not require discarding the dossier's main writing recommendations.

## Remaining uncertainty

No statistical reanalysis, model rerun, or longitudinal transfer test occurred. Accessible author manuscripts were distinguished from inaccessible publisher pages. The paper-to-skill implications remain proposals to test: the reviewed experiments do not establish the efficacy of the proposed writing workflow, a universal AI style deficit, or a detector-based definition of good prose.
