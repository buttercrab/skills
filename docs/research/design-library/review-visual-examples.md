# Independent sample review: visual examples

Reviewed 2026-11-07. Scope: six varied cards, their metadata/body/receipts and independently rendered public sources. This is a source/claim sample, not an audit of the whole catalog or acceptance testing of any product. No evaluation cases were read. The reviewer edited only this report; card corrections were delegated to the owning curator.

## Method

Used one owned Chrome tab through documented CUA APIs. All source visits were public HTTPS. Actual rendered screenshots were viewed at a desktop viewport approximately 1552 pixels wide. Public navigation and one visible language-selector interaction were exercised. No account, download, install, submission, financial action or other external write occurred. No assets were copied or downloaded. No local pages, browser source extraction, headless automation or alternate access surface was used. There were no policy denials. CUA work concluded with the completion summary “Reviewed six visual source receipts.”

The independent check separates three questions: whether a source opens, whether its viewed region supports the narrow observation, and whether the card limits transfer appropriately. Matching a pictured chart or character is not evidence of an effective product flow.

## Correction and residual limits

The only concrete defect found in this sample was Monument Valley's original direct source link. The reviewer notified the curator; the curator changed the link to the exact successful original entry and recorded fragile direct-route access rather than silently deleting the failure. The reviewer re-read the revised card and independently confirmed that original entry renders. Its remaining frame-specific title/cue observations retain the author's receipt and are not independently corroborated here.

Across the sample, metadata and prose preserve distinctions among public marketing composition, embedded phone imagery, an editorial index, a game-series presentation and a public language switch. None of the sampled cards presents those sources as measured efficacy evidence. Adaptation paragraphs make contextual recommendations, which remain design hypotheses to discuss with the person and test in their actual task.

This review does not establish accessibility, responsive quality, motion comfort, native-platform fidelity, translation quality or working product journeys. It samples only six of the visual cards; it does not independently verify the other ten, other library lanes, catalog behavior or the skill's end-to-end evaluation.
