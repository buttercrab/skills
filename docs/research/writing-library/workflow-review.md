Date: 2026-11-08 (Asia/Seoul). Read-only review of `writing-with-intent/SKILL.md`, all five current references, library FORMAT/VOCABULARY and the owned catalog helper. No assessment prompts/keys, curator research findings, source articles or other evaluation records were read. Active files and existing packages were not edited. ## Result

No material workflow, query or schema contradiction found. The instructions preserve author agency while allowing execution from a sufficient brief, narrow edit or explicit creative delegation. They do not mandate a style interview, example tour, fixed genre structure or recurring paragraph approval. Delayed answers supersede dependent proposals; sufficient direction leads to the requested draft/edit rather than another equivalent questionnaire.

One bounded quotation-wording clarification is worth making before future reuse:

- **FORMAT.md, paragraph beginning “Keep source-derived summary within source word limits.”** Its absolute “never exceed 25 words from a single non-lyrical source across artifacts” is a strict curation house limit as written. If intended to express copyright policy, scope it to the relevant non-user-provided copyrighted-source setting rather than imply the same restriction governs user-provided material or genuinely public-domain material. An alternative is to retain the conservative editorial preference for original annotations, explicitly call it that, and distinguish it from what supplied-draft editing permits. This review makes no public-domain determination about any particular work. The cap is currently confined to resource curation, so no refusal/truncation of a supplied draft is demonstrated.

## Observed coherence

- **Scope and artifact delivery:** the main skill names discussion/outline/draft/critique/limited edit/library as different artifacts; it explicitly rejects substituting an outline for a finished requested draft and keeps receipts/advice outside the prose. Example use returns lessons to the parent library rather than implicitly authorizing editing or publication of the source.
- **Author and reader decisions:** questions concern angle, stance, actual experience, evidence and consequential voice boundaries. The guide explicitly allows no-change limited edits and original provisional passages, without fabricated memories/quotes or forced choice after delegation. It distinguishes inspecting an example from endorsing it.
- **Genre and voice:** argument, narrative, explanation and reporting have different progressions. Short sentences, passive/active voice, headings, first person and repetition are contextual tools; Korean editing is not mechanically governed by English house rules. The whole-piece review does not expand the authorized editing layer.
- **Evidence versus craft:** published structure, reported author process, actual revision artifacts and empirical reader effects remain separate. Context/entity/denominator/causal qualifiers survive editing. Article quality and AI authorship are not collapsed; tool flags and historical study findings do not certify truth, originality or a universal AI deficit. No intervention-effect claim is made for this skill.
- **Source and reuse:** whole-piece structure claims require corresponding access; inaccessible/partial content remains qualified. The core and library-usage guide specify general techniques rather than living-author voice cloning, brief source quotation/linking rather than article reproduction, and no automatic source contact/upload/install/publishing authority. The generated catalog footer expresses the same lack of automatic authorization rather than a requirement to seek repeated approvals.
- **Query interface:** supplied examples match the actual CLI. `--root` precedes the subcommand; list/find/facets support kind/status/availability and task/genre/language/context. All literal terms and repeated facet values use AND; alternative kind/status/availability values use OR within a filter. Exact facet labels and matched fields are visible; there is no hidden quality/authorship ranking.
- **Schema:** kinds and facet keys align across FORMAT, helper and vocabulary. Only example cards additionally require substantive Craft trace and Revision implications. Optional documented-flow, visual and interaction receipts use the documented URL/locator/sequence/observed/actions/method fields; interaction requires flow as well as interaction receipts. These structural declarations are not claimed as source truth or reader validation.

## Limits

This is an integration review of guidance and interface semantics, not a behavioral assessment, source-article audit, legal review, author-satisfaction study or empirical validation. It does not establish that future agents will follow the workflow. The earlier 24 helper tests concern temporary-fixture structure, filtering, Unicode and portability; they are not writing-quality acceptance tests. No wording assertions or extra mandatory checklist were added.
