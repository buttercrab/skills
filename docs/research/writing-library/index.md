# Writing library research

Inspected 2026-11-08. The deliverable is [Writing with Intent](../../../skills/writing-with-intent/SKILL.md), with a [browseable library](../../../skills/writing-with-intent/library/index.md) and [reading paths](../../../skills/writing-with-intent/library/PATHS.md). The agent asks about consequential author choices, selects contextual craft mechanisms, and delivers actual prose. Clear briefs and narrow edits do not require another interview.

## Coverage and evidence

[Machine-readable coverage](coverage.json) records 43 original annotated resources: 14 guides, 14 examples, 10 study cards, three optional tools and two foundations. They cite 56 distinct source URLs and contain 8,324 body words, with 86 related-card links. All cards are curated; 42 have public access and one has explicitly partial access. These are counts of resources and locations, not independent confirmations, a systematic review or a census of good writing.

Four research lanes used actual primary bodies, specified sections, methods/results or pinned diffs:

| Lane | Work and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [Article and revision examples](examples.md) | Twelve articles/revision cases across explanation, personal essay, reported narrative and argument. Two source families expose actual before/after patches. Editorial interpretations are distinct from author motives and reader evidence. |
| [Craft and structure](craft.md) | Eleven audience, argument, paragraph, information-order, revision and contextual voice resources. Advice can conflict across genres; no universal short-sentence or active-voice mandate. |
| [AI-writing evidence](ai-evidence.md) | Ten cards from eleven primary papers. Benefits, diversity, culture, agency, factuality and detector limits use different measures and populations. Historical models and bounded tasks do not establish current universal quality. |
| [Editorial practice and tools](practice.md) | Ten cards covering reporting/corrections, Korean public language, bilingual technical writing, attribution/worked paraphrase and three optional tools. Source/configuration inspection only; upstream tools were not installed or executed. |

The example total includes twelve from the article lane and two worked/bilingual examples from practice. The core references add three original synthetic micro-edits. No full third-party articles or paper bodies are bundled.

## Independent source sampling

Four cross-lane reviews checked eight selected cards against primary material: [examples](review-examples.md), [AI evidence](review-ai-evidence.md), [craft](review-craft.md), and [practice guides](review-practice-guides.md). This is a sample, not verification of every claim in all 43 cards. A separate [workflow/schema review](workflow-review.md) inspected integration.

Corrections retained in the package include distinguishing explanation of an existing simplifying assumption from introduction of a new one in the Distill patch; identifying Noy/Zhang's inspected manuscript and incentive-arm boundary instead of merging versions; and keeping historical detector results separate from authorship certainty. Language/reader facets were normalized without changing study populations. The curation quotation preference does not restrict editing user-supplied drafts.

## Access, maintenance and remaining work

The poetry/source-label card is partial: only inspected Study 1 findings are used; detailed Study 2 estimates remain withheld. Blocked informational source-label and cognition leads were not promoted from an older dossier. Gawande is an opening-only example, not a whole-article structural analysis. AP guidance is dated; the Korean official guide is from 2014. Lane receipts name other blocked routes and exact inspection limits.

English prose and short experimental writing tasks dominate. Future research should broaden languages, genres and author/reader contexts, recover specific blocked methods/results, and retain negative or conflicting findings. Refresh the particular changing source/version rather than blending old and new estimates. New resources must follow the [card format](../../../skills/writing-with-intent/library/FORMAT.md), use inspected bodies and explicit transfer limits, then rebuild and validate the catalog.

The [development evaluation](../../evaluations/writing-library/results.md) uses six actual prose runs across five independently authored fictional cases. A separate [fresh v1.1 follow-up](../../evaluations/writing-library/followup-2026-11-08/results.md) covers an unanswered-author case under clarified trial permissions. Neither cohort measures human satisfaction, literary merit, reader comprehension or causal improvement from the skill. This work stays in the reusable library; no Taster or other example-project changes are part of this writing task.
