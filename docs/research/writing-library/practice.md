# Writing library: practice curation

Inspected 2026-11-08. This lane owns `practice-*.md`: source integrity, Korean/English editorial context, worked revision examples, and optional lint tools. Ten curated cards; these are original selection annotations, not redistributed source bodies. No assessment fixtures were read. No upstream tools, tests, scripts, packages, hooks or publication actions were run. Local Python was used only to write and validate card metadata.

## Decisions that should affect the skill

- Ask which statements come from the writer's actual experience and which are hypothetical. Do not produce personal testimony to make an article feel human.
- Ask which publication/genre and language relationship apply before enforcing a house style. A Korean public-service announcement, conversational engineering retrospective and English reported article have different contracts.
- Keep observed fact, attributed statement, interpretation and hypothetical illustration distinguishable. This four-part drafting aid is our synthesis, not a named upstream standard.
- An empirical study, institutional policy, craft interpretation and lint flag are different kinds of evidence. The dated AP policy is not an experiment about AI writing quality. A company's retrospective is not an independently reproduced benchmark.
- Do not erase uncertainty to satisfy a confidence/hedging check. The strength of a sentence must follow evidence.
- A factual correction requires a reader-visible account of what changed. Line editing cannot silently resolve source gaps.
- A user should choose the relevant conventions. Tools remain optional and need separate authorization/context before installing, enabling hooks, obtaining style packages or executing scripts.
