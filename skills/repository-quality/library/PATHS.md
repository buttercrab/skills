# Reading paths by maintenance problem

Use these as starting contrasts, then inspect the cards and the relevant primary sections. They do not prescribe a fixed shortlist or replace context discovery. [Browse all entries](index.md) or inspect actual labels with `python3 scripts/catalog.py facets --status curated`.

| Maintenance problem | Concrete cases | Guidance / contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A small tree seems “too simple” | [p-limit](entries/repo-small-p-limit.md), [Flask](entries/repo-small-flask.md) | Compare actual exports/imports and consumer boundary through [npm artifacts](entries/quality-npm-artifact-exports.md) or [Python layouts](entries/quality-python-import-layout.md), not folder count. |
| A new contributor cannot find the right change | [Click](entries/repo-small-click.md), [Cobra](entries/repo-small-cobra.md), [Django](entries/repo-large-django.md) | [Change questions study](entries/docs-study-change-questions.md) and [contribution entry](entries/docs-github-contribution-entry.md); preserve optional environments and routing. |
| Generated output and source disagree | [uv](entries/repo-small-uv.md), [Testify](entries/repo-small-testify.md), [SQLite](entries/repo-large-sqlite.md), [Go](entries/repo-large-go.md) | [Source authority](entries/quality-generated-source-authority.md); inspect what is checked in, regenerated at build, or published. |
| Source-tree tests pass but packaging is uncertain | [p-limit](entries/repo-small-p-limit.md), [Click](entries/repo-small-click.md), [bat](entries/repo-small-bat.md) | [Python distribution checks](entries/quality-python-distribution-checks.md), [npm artifact exports](entries/quality-npm-artifact-exports.md), [Cargo packaging](entries/quality-cargo-package-boundary.md). |
| Workspace structure is being confused with releases | [Kubernetes](entries/repo-large-kubernetes.md), [VS Code](entries/repo-large-vscode.md), [ripgrep](entries/repo-small-ripgrep.md) | [Go modules/workspaces](entries/quality-go-module-workspace-boundaries.md), [npm workspace scope](entries/quality-npm-workspace-scope.md), [Cargo workspace selection](entries/quality-cargo-workspace-selection.md). |
| Documentation is hard to maintain or contribute | [MDN](entries/docs-mdn-content-contracts.md), [Rust book](entries/docs-rust-book-source-listings.md), [Astro docs](entries/docs-astro-localized-contracts.md) | [Diataxis](entries/docs-diataxis-reader-needs.md) for reader needs, [docs as code](entries/docs-docs-as-code.md) for workflow; shared tooling is not proof of clear or correct writing. |
| A score, badge or process is being treated as quality proof | [Coverage study](entries/docs-study-coverage-limits.md), [Google review study](entries/docs-study-google-review.md) | [Scorecard limits](entries/quality-scorecard-diagnostic-limits.md), [CODEOWNERS](entries/docs-github-codeowners.md), [SLSA](entries/quality-slsa-provenance-verification.md): separate heuristic, configured mechanism and observed outcome. |
| Release overhead is growing | [Release planning workflows](entries/quality-release-planning-workflows.md) | [Versioning and human notes](entries/quality-versioning-and-human-release-notes.md), [reproducibility](entries/quality-reproducible-build-evidence.md); no publication workflow is required for every internal script. |

Related-resource IDs in cards connect mechanisms across scale and resource type; they are suggested reading, not independent corroboration or automatic activation of upstream instructions.
