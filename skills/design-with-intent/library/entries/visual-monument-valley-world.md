# Monument Valley III: a coherent geometric world

## When to use

Use to discuss a coherent creative world when the subject genuinely supports atmosphere, spatial imagination or narrative. It can help a person identify relationships among palette, geometry, character and composition.

## What to inspect

Inspect the loaded official third-game hero and its surrounding series navigation. The linked root entry produced the client-rendered /mv3 view in this inspection. The visual evidence is website artwork/video presentation, not a played puzzle or installed game.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow coherence rather than the recognizable architecture. A cultural experience or fictional world can develop original shape, scale and color rules that reinforce its premise. Ask which relationship matters to the person: mystery, quietness, spatial play or character scale.

Adapt navigation to the real task. An expressive world can frame an experience while controls and explanations remain understandable. Consider reduced motion and alternative access when imagery or animation carries important information.
