# Zotero: annotations that retain their source context

## When to use

Use for evidence collection, reading clubs or editorial tools where extracted material must remain traceable. The fit is the relationship between a note and its origin, not a generic requirement for a three-pane workspace.

## What to inspect

Read the difference between one-off selection and locked repeated highlighting, then how annotations enter notes and return to their source. External readers have a stated page-opening limitation.

## What to borrow and adapt

Keep source identity, location and the person’s interpretation available as material moves into a synthesis. In this design library, an example selection should retain the exact screen/state or section that justified the match. Let people combine notes without converting their source provenance into an untraceable pasted paragraph. Choose repeated-mode tools only when their efficiency outweighs the risk of accidental marks.

## Mismatches and limits

The documentation was read; no reader, annotation or sync behavior was exercised. A citation link does not validate a claim or guarantee the external target still opens at the right place. Copying quotations needs its own reuse judgment. Compare Google Docs for proposed edits rather than evidence capture.
