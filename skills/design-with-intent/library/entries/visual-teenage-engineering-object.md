# teenage engineering: physical product as the visual center

## When to use

Use when material, industrial form and the actual artifact should establish a product's character. It is a contrast to software pages that rely on abstract gradient fields or stock lifestyle imagery.

## What to inspect

Inspect the loaded device photograph and the navigation's category icons, fine rules and compact secondary labels. The screenshot covers one live homepage presentation. Store controls, regional pricing and checkout were not operated or evaluated.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the alignment between object and visual language. A workshop, instrument maker or physical-product archive can choose imagery and graphic detail that reveal material and construction. Ask which real features the owner wants people to notice before choosing an unrelated decorative style.

Adapt legibility and hierarchy to the audience and device. Fine linework and small technical labels can create character while also creating reading or target-size problems. Preserve the task's clarity rather than copying density for its own sake.

## Mismatches and limits

No product performance, purchasing behavior, responsive layout or accessibility was tested. The photograph and icon system are proprietary assets, not templates supplied by this card. This composition can be a poor fit for an urgent service form or a content-heavy community whose people matter more than one object.
