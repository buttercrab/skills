# Pinterest Gestalt: investigation candidate

## When to use

Keep this as a candidate when broadening a shortlist beyond enterprise and government systems toward a consumer-product organization. It is a place to investigate, not yet a recommendation for a specific Pinterest-like design or behavior.

## What to inspect

The public landing describes a shared Pinterest design language and links to foundations, components and getting started. The deeper overview routes did not expose enough substantive guidance in this research pass. Before promotion, inspect actual decision guidance, supported implementations, component states and a representative screen through an approved public surface.

## What to borrow and adapt

The transferable next step is a focused comparison question: does the current system offer guidance relevant to the user’s actual discovery, saving or organization task? Ask the user which of those tasks and what expressive direction matter, then inspect relevant material. Record observed evidence rather than inferring a visual style from Pinterest’s reputation.

## Mismatches and limits

No source-supported claim about component quality, visual character, motion or accessibility is made. Only the landing and route availability were examined; no component, native product or flow was tested. This explicit candidate preserves the research gap so an agent will not quietly substitute name recognition for evidence. Compare curated systems while deeper access remains unresolved.
