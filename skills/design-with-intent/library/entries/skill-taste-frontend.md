# tasteskill: business-type marketing production kit

## When to use

Select this as a reference for making a company or product site carry real subject matter rather than empty decorative sections. Its distinction between a place people visit, a service they hire and software they operate can prompt useful questions about content and the primary action.

## What to inspect

Read the business-type routing and the actual production checklist before considering the kit. Inspect the required assets, Node/browser dependencies and reference files for the chosen route. The entrypoint describes executable layout dealers, photographs, checker scripts and performance checks; their successful behavior was not tested here.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the demand that a product promise be supported by relevant material and that the page explain what is offered. Make asset gaps explicit and preserve the client’s actual content. Adapt the concept of checking every intended viewport to the supported platform and the task’s evidence needs.

## Mismatches and limits

One-section-per-screen, banned violet, mandated hover behavior, photographic quotas and fixed layout treatments are a strong house style. They may contradict reading, comparison, accessibility, client-brand or expressive objectives. The source also allows plausible local details in some draft paths; do not turn those into factual claims about a real business. No scripts or assets were installed or executed.
