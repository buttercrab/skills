# Mobbin: screen and flow discovery with access limits

## When to use

Select Mobbin as a discovery route when the local library lacks relevant shipped app or site references. It can point a researcher toward consumer/mobile contexts that are underrepresented in public design-system documentation.

## What to inspect

Inspect the available search categories and the actual authorized example, screen sequence, platform and date. The public product page was read here; full subscription content, search results and member-only flow bodies were not accessed. Verify access before proposing a task that depends on them.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the practice of searching by a concrete job or interaction rather than a fashionable style label. Compare neighboring products and preserve the exact state or sequence being used. A selected example can suggest what to inspect in the original product, while the human explains which qualities fit their own audience and intent.

## Mismatches and limits

The public marketing page and its collection claims do not establish the contents or quality of every reference. No screenshots were visually inspected in this card, and no recorded flow was tested. Do not assert that an unavailable example matches the brief, infer present behavior from an old capture, bypass paid access, or redistribute product screenshots. This is a curated discovery-tool entry with partial access, not a vetted recommendation for particular unseen screens.
