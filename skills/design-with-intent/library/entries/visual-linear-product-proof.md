# Linear: dense product proof within a restrained landing page

## When to use

Use when a professional product needs to show substantive working context rather than rely entirely on abstract promises. It is a visual reference for framing dense software evidence within a relatively quiet page.

## What to inspect

Inspect the loaded hero and the issue/activity representation: navigation, content hierarchy, discussion, state and adjacent work pane. The first capture was still loading; the card is based on the later visible composition. This scene is marketing presentation, not an authenticated product session.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the balance between a simple outer message and a detailed inner example. An operational tool can show realistic relationships among the records, people and decisions it supports. Choose a representative task with honest content and explain why its details matter.

Adapt density and scale to the intended reader. A complex screenshot reduced beyond legibility becomes decoration. Product proof should expose a meaningful structure, while the surrounding page helps a newcomer understand where to look.

## Mismatches and limits

This does not establish current workflow correctness, agent capability, performance, accessibility or user productivity. Dark surfaces, gray borders and spacious marketing margins are contextual choices. Copying the styling without the underlying information model would not reproduce the design's value. No app actions were performed.
