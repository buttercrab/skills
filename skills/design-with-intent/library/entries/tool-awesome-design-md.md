# Awesome DESIGN.md: discovery through interpreted design documents

## When to use

Select this as a discovery collection when the person wants design-language references that can be read by an agent. It can surface brands and descriptive axes worth investigating, especially for developer-focused marketing work.

## What to inspect

Read the directory’s category and then the actual selected document, checking whether it is an interpretation, an official source or a reconstruction. Follow the candidate into the original product and its relevant screen before making a visual-match claim. This inspection sampled the Voltagent-inspired document, not the whole collection.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the idea of turning a reference into role-based, implementation-relevant notes. Separate the visual role from exact values and identify the particular quality the human wants. Treat the directory as an invitation to compare sources: a similar palette may be less important than the requested content hierarchy or trust cues.

## Mismatches and limits

The sampled document explicitly labels itself inspired interpretation. Its numeric tokens do not prove the original product’s canonical system, current values or intended rationale. Directory descriptions and badges are not efficacy evidence. No source website or screenshots were visually inspected for this card, so do not use it alone to certify visual similarity or copy branded assets. No files were installed into a project.
