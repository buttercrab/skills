# Gentler Streak: a companion alongside a mobile data view

## When to use

Use when exploring a friendly interpretation layer around personal data. The useful distinction is that a character and a chart can have separate roles within one screen.

## What to inspect

Inspect the embedded phone, not only the outdoor hero photograph. Notice the hierarchy from companion illustration to greeting and interpretation, then chart and concrete activity. The navigation and chart are visible in a product image; they were not operated.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the relationship between data and a concise human-readable interpretation. A learning-progress or creative-practice tool might show what a trend means before asking someone to inspect individual points. An original character can support the tone if the owner wants that relationship, while the data and available actions remain explicit.

Adapt uncertainty, units and consequences to the actual domain. Do not reuse a reassuring label when the underlying state is unknown. Keep the chart legible and offer needed detail independently of decorative character work.
