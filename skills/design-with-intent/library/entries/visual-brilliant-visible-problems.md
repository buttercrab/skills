# Brilliant: make the learning material visible

## When to use

Use when the subject matter can demonstrate the product more meaningfully than stock photos or generic benefits. It is a reference for making a learning proposition concrete through examples of the actual material.

## What to inspect

Inspect the current hero, differentiated audience actions and mathematical panels. The viewed page emphasizes tutoring and visible problem representations; do not substitute a remembered older version of the brand. No problem was solved or lesson opened.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow subject-specific visual evidence: show the kind of question, object or transformation people will work with. For a creative tool, that could be a real editing example; for a community workshop, an actual activity or artifact. Keep examples intelligible at the displayed scale.

Adapt the composition to the information's structure. A fan of panels can imply range but may become decorative if every panel is unreadable. Ask which material should be understood immediately and which can serve as atmosphere.

## Mismatches and limits

The screenshot does not verify tutoring quality, learning outcomes, interaction, device behavior or audience suitability. Mathematical imagery must remain correct if adapted; invented equations are not harmless filler. The brand's serif choice and panel arrangement are not universal requirements for education.
