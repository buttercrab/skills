# Headspace: illustrated content beside a human service offer

## When to use

Use when one product family contains meaningfully different kinds of help or service. It is a visual reference for separating propositions while retaining a recognizable brand.

## What to inspect

Inspect the two hero panels and their imagery, headings and actions. The app panel combines illustration with a phone-content preview; the service panel uses a person on a video-call screen. Inspect the actual current composition rather than assuming the brand consists only of orange characters.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the use of distinct evidence for distinct offers. A community product might separate self-guided resources from a hosted event, using an appropriate image and action for each. Ask whether visitors already understand that distinction; if not, the content and hierarchy must explain it.

Adapt the number of choices and their prominence to the user's task. The shared panel treatment can connect related offers without pretending that their commitments are identical.

## Mismatches and limits

Only the public marketing composition was viewed. App use, clinical intake, coverage checking, pricing and outcomes were not tested. No medical efficacy or endorsement is asserted. Rounded forms and friendly imagery do not establish trustworthiness or accessibility. Do not copy clinical claims, ratings, photography or proprietary artwork into an unrelated product.
