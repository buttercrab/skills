# Kagi: character beside a search-first surface

## When to use

Use when a tool should have personality while one utilitarian action remains dominant. It is a useful counterexample to treating a mascot as requiring an entire cartoon world.

## What to inspect

Inspect the relationship between wordmark, line character, query field and example-query chips. The public landing surface was viewed without submitting a query, signing in or changing account settings. Dark presentation is the observed state, not a claim about every available theme.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the limited role of illustration and the prominence of the core task. A reference finder or community directory can express identity near a search box while keeping actual input and examples straightforward. Examples should demonstrate useful queries rather than act as filler.

Adapt the character's voice and complexity to the audience. Compare this restrained use with Duolingo's cast and Finch's landscape when a person is deciding what “friendly” should mean for their product.

## Mismatches and limits

No search result, ranking, privacy, subscription, speed or accessibility claim was tested. A search-first surface is inappropriate when people need orientation or comparison before they can formulate a query. Do not copy the dog, logo, example content or unverifiable marketing claims as assets or facts.
