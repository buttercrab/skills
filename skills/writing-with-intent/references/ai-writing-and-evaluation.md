# AI-assisted writing: diagnose the work and evaluate the claim

Use this for “AI-like” prose critiques or claims about assistance, creativity, quality and authorship. The [study cards](../library/index.md) preserve specific models, versions, tasks, populations, measures and limitations. Neither AI origin nor human origin settles writing quality.

## Different questions need different evidence

| Question | Inspect | Avoid inferring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this piece useful and well argued? | Reader/author brief, actual claims/examples, structure, evidence and voice | An authorship verdict from punctuation, polish or vocabulary. |
| Did assistance help a bounded task? | Original task, treatment, sample, comparison and measured outcome | The same benefit for professional long-form work, all writers or current models. |
| Did many assisted outputs become more alike? | Corpus/task/model and the actual diversity measure | Every individual output is bad or no assisted writer can be original. |
| Does a source label affect readers? | Genre, framing manipulation, awareness and response measures | Expert literary merit or a contradiction across incomparable studies. |
| Can a detector identify authorship reliably here? | Test population, model/version, error distribution and context | That a score is truth or an editorial acceptance criterion. |
| Are factual claims supported? | Claim/source/context/entity relationships and meaningful inference | Fluent prose or a single factuality metric proves the whole argument. |

Benefits and failures can coexist under different measures. Distinguish writing speed, reviewer ratings, factual accuracy, individual creativity, collective diversity, perceived authenticity and author agency. A study can illuminate a risk without validating this skill's intervention. Historical models and detector populations are not current universal rankings.

## Repair observable failures

- **Interchangeable subject:** clarify the actual reader/claim and use a true example specific to this piece.
- **Unsupported certainty or abstraction:** inspect evidence, add the missing reasoning or narrow the claim; do not merely add a citation after it.
- **Repetition without development:** keep orientation where useful and make each retained passage advance the piece.
- **False intimacy:** ask for a real author detail or use an explicitly fictional illustration; do not invent personal history.
- **Flattened voice:** recover the author's stance, register and meaningful particularity instead of injecting arbitrary quirks.

These are diagnoses of text and process, not a fingerprint list. No universal ban on phrases, long sentences, em dashes, headings or passive voice follows. Do not add typos or fake memories to make a detector return a desired score.

## A proportional final review

Check intent fidelity, supported substance, whole-piece reasoning, coherent progression, precise meaning, appropriate voice/genre and completion of the requested artifact. Use actual source checks and reader feedback when available, identifying their scope. Keep factual errors, weak inferences, unmet author direction, scope drift and subjective editorial alternatives distinct.

An independent reader can reveal confusion; the author still controls the desired stance. An agent-only developmental trial can expose missed constraints or fabricated details but cannot establish genuine reader delight, literary merit or that questioning improves creativity. Do not aggregate those into a single quality score.

## Maintain the library from evidence

Use [FORMAT](../library/FORMAT.md) to curate canonical cards; regenerate rather than hand-edit indexes. Preserve exact sources/sections/version, whole-versus-partial access, evidence class, transfer/mismatch and attribution. Actual revisions require actual artifacts. New leads remain candidates until the relevant body/method is inspected. Do not refresh a date as a substitute for source review.

A bounded trial should return a narrow supported lesson to the skill/library, not expand into editing or publishing the example. Keep prior case/package/review snapshots and later wording distinct. Exposed examples are development material; comparisons need fresh tasks/contexts and appropriate controls. No scheduled refresh, detector submission, installation or publishing is implied.
