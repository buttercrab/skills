# Reading paths by editorial decision

Use these as starting contrasts, not a mandatory reading list or a ranking of authors. [Browse all entries](index.md), inspect relevant cards and follow the necessary primary passages. An article can teach craft without establishing its factual claims or reader benefit.

| Decision | Examples / practice | Contrasts and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a specific angle or reader question | [Stranger-reader test](entries/example-words-reader-test.md), [promise reframing](entries/example-ten-years-reframe-promise.md) | [Audience](entries/craft-google-audience.md), [theme](entries/craft-jack-hart-theme.md); a forceful essay's universal claim is not empirical fact. |
| Explain a difficult object or missing bridge | [Inside .git](entries/example-inside-git-inspectable-objects.md), [momentum revision](entries/example-momentum-revision-reader-questions.md), [word-frequency revision](entries/example-word-frequencies-revision-consistency.md) | [Explanation gaps](entries/craft-julia-evans-explanation-gaps.md), [information order](entries/craft-gopen-swan-information-order.md); commands/math were not independently validated by craft reading. |
| Make evidence support the actual argument | [Definition before causes](entries/example-life-expectancy-definition-before-causes.md), [reported mathematical explanation](entries/example-hyperbolic-surfaces-reported-explanation.md) | [Argument](entries/craft-unc-argument.md), [Reuters sourcing](entries/practice-reuters-sourcing.md), [claim/context](entries/evidence-claim-verification.md) and [entity coherence](entries/evidence-entity-coherence.md). |
| Revise structure rather than swap synonyms | [Actual momentum diff](entries/example-momentum-revision-reader-questions.md), [actual prerequisite propagation diff](entries/example-word-frequencies-revision-consistency.md) | [Reverse outline](entries/craft-wisconsin-reverse-outline.md), [paragraph development](entries/craft-unc-paragraph-development.md); only actual artifacts support a before/after history. |

| Use an automated editorial aid | [Vale](entries/practice-vale.md), [proselint](entries/practice-proselint.md), [write-good](entries/practice-write-good.md) | Local rule/configuration flags are prompts for review, not truth, quality or authorship verdicts. No tool was run in curation. |

A no-match result can be useful. Keep the author's direction, describe the limited transfer or missing sample, and broaden primary discovery when needed. More references are not automatically better writing.
