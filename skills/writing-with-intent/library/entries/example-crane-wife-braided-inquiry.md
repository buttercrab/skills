## When to use

When two genuine experiences illuminate the same unresolved question. Ask the writer what they are still uncertain about; do not supply a trauma narrative for them.

## What to inspect

The alternating expedition and relationship scenes, the resource-counting hinge and the final action instead of a detached moral.

## Craft trace

Question: what does it mean to acknowledge needs? Concrete work in one strand supplies a way to reconsider choices in the other. Later scenes complicate a simple account of departure; the ending makes care observable through an action.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: annotate what each strand newly enables the reader to understand. Cut a switch that merely repeats the theme; keep uncomfortable details only when the author chooses their disclosure and they change the inquiry.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow functional alternation, a shared question and an earned concrete ending. Let the author choose their own experiences, imagery and degree of intimacy.

## Mismatches and limits

A neat braid can force a false equivalence between unrelated experiences. This is testimony and interpretation, not independent reporting about other people or a general psychological diagnosis.
