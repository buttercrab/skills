## When to use

When readers arrive with a seductive but underspecified promise, such as instant mastery.

## What to inspect

The breakdown of a book-title promise, distinction between familiarity and expertise, explicit qualification of a single timescale, then concrete alternatives.

## Craft trace

Question: what can the advertised shortcut actually deliver? The argument disassembles its terms before proposing a longer practice path. The qualification prevents a memorable numerical frame from becoming an exact law.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: define what success would mean at each claimed timescale. Replace a rejection with a feasible next activity and feedback loop. Audit whether a catchy heading overstates the evidence beneath it.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow term-by-term analysis and an actionable alternative. Select respectful language appropriate to the user’s reader rather than copying the source’s dismissive moments.

## Mismatches and limits

This is not current educational advice or validation of a universal expertise threshold. Linked research, tools, timings and book recommendations require separate checking; a strong counterpromise can also oversimplify.
