## When to use

A technical article alternates between a procedure, background explanation and reference details without helping readers locate what they need. Ask whether the immediate purpose is learning a skill, applying it, understanding it, or consulting information.

## What to inspect

Inspect the two dimensions rather than merely copying the four category names. The compass can diagnose a passage as well as a whole document; its terminology is a means of discussing reader needs.

## What to borrow and adapt

For an article about image caching, separate a guided first experiment from the explanation of why invalidation works. The experiment can link to conceptual background at the point of need, while a lookup table can remain available for returning readers. If the intended article is a reflective account of discovering a bug, retain its narrative purpose instead of recasting it as a how-to.

## Mismatches and limits

This is a documentation framework, not a universal taxonomy of articles, essays or literature. Mixed forms can be deliberate and useful; the task is to manage transitions and expectations, not require four files or categories. Only the compass page was inspected for this card. No claim is made about the whole framework's empirical effectiveness or an implementation audit of a particular documentation site.
