# 국립국어원: Korean public writing has a specific reader contract

## When to use
Korean guidance must help a general audience understand an explanation, eligibility rule, procedure, or institutional announcement.

## What to inspect
The guide addresses public institutions speaking to citizens. Its examples examine reader relationships, unfamiliar terminology, sentence-component agreement, missing actors, noun sequences, and distinctions between duties, discretion and exceptions.

## What to borrow and adapt
Ask who must act, on what, by when, and under which exception. In a Korean draft, restore an omitted actor or object when context cannot recover it; do not mechanically add pronouns to every sentence. Define necessary technical terms for the intended readers. Ask affected people about preferred descriptions when terminology carries social judgments. Decide the speech level and relationship deliberately before applying a consistency pass.

## Mismatches and limits
This is a 2014 public-language guide, not a rule for all Korean essays, dialogue, fiction, specialist engineering prose, or contemporary law. Its historical term replacements need fresh contextual review. Preserve expert terms when replacement would reduce precision. Neither English brevity scores nor a blanket ban on foreign words can decide Korean article quality.
