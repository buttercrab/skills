## When to use

When an explanatory article uses a measure readers recognize but may interpret incorrectly.

## What to inspect

The initial subquestions, definition before decomposition, age-group sections and return to changes across time.

## Craft trace

Question: what explains an observed population difference? The article first narrows what its metric measures, then divides the explanation into contributions rather than treating the gap as one undifferentiated cause.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: write the most plausible reader misinterpretation next to the metric. Add the definition where that misconception first matters. Separate description, decomposition and causal interpretation in the draft.

## What to borrow and adapt

Choose section units that can answer different parts of the original question. Keep changes of population or period explicit, and connect chart claims to the exact comparison.

## Mismatches and limits

This is craft analysis, not medical advice or independent confirmation of biological explanations. Interactive behavior and the correctness of all underlying studies remain untested; population summaries cannot predict an individual.
