## When to use

When an explanation jumps from one vivid case directly to a favorite cause.

## What to inspect

Only the opening comparison and successive alternative explanations.

## Craft trace

Question: why did two innovations spread differently? Paired histories create a puzzle; successive counterexamples complicate easy explanations before the proposed distinction appears.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: list two plausible rival explanations and the evidence that would distinguish them. Revise the lead so it earns the question before it names the answer.

## What to borrow and adapt

Build a comparison around comparable evidence; seek exceptions before committing to a causal frame.

## Mismatches and limits

Historical analogy alone cannot establish causality. The card covers an opening, not the article’s complete structure or the effectiveness of clinical interventions.
