## When to use

When a conceptual essay has an authentic place or practice from which to ask its question.

## What to inspect

The movement from gathering berries to language, exchange and a neighbor’s farm; note the late qualification about operating alongside markets.

## Craft trace

Question: what changes when exchange is understood through relationship? Sensory observation gives the argument a situation; attributed conversation tests the analogy against practical costs. The ending proposes a direction rather than presenting a controlled comparison.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: label each paragraph as observation, interpretation, borrowed claim or proposal. Add evidence where a metaphor starts doing empirical work. Seek a real case whose complications can change the argument.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow movement across scales and a counterpressure from material constraints. Preserve cultural attribution and the author’s relationship to the subject.

## Mismatches and limits

Indigenous knowledge and terminology are not decorative material for an unrelated voice. An ecological analogy does not by itself validate an economic policy; broad scientific and economic propositions need separate verification.
