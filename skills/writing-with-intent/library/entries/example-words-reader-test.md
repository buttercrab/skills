## When to use

When an argument feels clear to its author but its audience cannot reconstruct the reasoning.

## What to inspect

The move from a familiar drafting difficulty to an unfamiliar reader, followed by domain qualifications and the concluding proposition.

## Craft trace

Question: why does explaining an apparently formed idea change it? The essay treats imagined reader incomprehension as pressure to make missing connections explicit. Qualifications test the scope before the final claim expands it.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: mark each inference that assumes shared background. Ask a reader to explain the connection; revise the reasoning before polishing the sentence. Keep only qualifications that change the claim.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the reader test, not an imitation of the essay’s cadence or the author’s writing routine. State what evidence would disconfirm the draft’s central proposition.

## Mismatches and limits

Personal observation and analogy do not prove a universal theory of thinking. An expert memo may legitimately rely on shared concepts that a general reader lacks.
