## When to use

Use when someone equates AI origin with poor writing or reader enjoyment with artistic excellence. Ask who the readers are and what they are judging: accessibility, feeling, credibility, originality or expert craft.

## What to inspect

The study compared five selected poems by each of ten established English-language poets with the first five ChatGPT-3.5 imitations per poet. Study 1 involved 1,634 mostly non-expert readers, with identification accuracy below chance. Study 2 recruited 696 readers and randomized labels independently of actual authorship. Those designs distinguish source framing from the text presented.

## What to borrow and adapt

Our application: define the target reader and evaluation question before comparing drafts. A blind comparison can address the reading experience, while a separate discussion addresses transparency or expectations. Explain why a user prefers a passage in concrete terms instead of treating that preference as proof of origin.

## Mismatches and limits

These sampled poems and readers do not establish article quality, expert literary merit or present-model capability. No imitations are supplied here. Detailed label-effect estimates and perceived-originality exceptions require re-access before quantitative use; broad findings from the earlier dossier are not silently substituted. This card is strongest for the inspected Study 1 result and framing design.
