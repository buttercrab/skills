# AP 2023: generated material needs an accountable source

## When to use
An agent contributes factual prose, source suggestions, images, or summaries to a nonfiction draft.

## What to inspect
The dated AP guidance treats generated output as unvetted source material, retains journalist accountability, and separates depicting a real event from presenting generated art as a labeled subject.

## What to borrow and adapt
Create a claim ledger before line editing: assertion, actual inspected source, exact supporting passage, date, uncertainty, and owner. An agent's confident explanation cannot fill an empty source cell. If a source cannot be inspected, narrow the claim or state the access gap. Ask the writer which experiences and judgments are theirs rather than fabricating personal testimony. Check the applicable publication's current AI policy separately.

## Mismatches and limits
AP's 2023 restriction on using ChatGPT to create publishable content is its policy at that date, not a universal prohibition on assistance. This article does not measure detection accuracy, factual error rates, or literary quality. Do not infer authorship from polished language or label a writer's voice as machine-generated. Privacy and publication decisions depend on the actual material and agreed workflow.
