## When to use

Use for a polished argument recommending a decision. Ask what would make the recommendation wrong even if the prose remains persuasive.

## What to inspect

The experiment involved 758 consultants overall and compared no AI, GPT-4, and GPT-4 plus an overview. Assistance helped on tasks designated within the capability frontier. On a deliberately selected outside-frontier task, AI users were less often correct. Independent raters nevertheless gave AI-assisted recommendations higher Recommendation Quality scores, including incorrect recommendations.

## What to borrow and adapt

Our application: create separate review passes for evidence, inference, and expression. Identify the decisive calculation or premise first; then inspect whether the recommendation follows. A recommendation that reads well must still be rejected or qualified when the underlying decision is unsupported.

## Mismatches and limits

Recommendation Quality is a composite task measure, not an isolated test of prose style. One model, consultant population and constructed frontier do not establish current task rankings. The approximately 19-percentage-point correctness gap belongs to the outside-frontier comparison, not all writing. This study does not test our proposed review sequence.
