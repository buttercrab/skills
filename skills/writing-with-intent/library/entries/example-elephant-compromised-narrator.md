## When to use

When a reflective essay needs to examine the writer’s participation rather than assign every failure to other people.

## What to inspect

The opening institutional conflict, the point when the crowd changes the narrator’s options and the last explanation of motive.

## Craft trace

Question: how can someone with formal authority lose practical agency? The event progressively exposes the narrator’s investment in appearances; the ending revises the reader’s judgment of his earlier explanations.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: identify where the writer benefits from the account they are giving. Place the relevant choice in a scene; then distinguish what the writer knew then from what they claim now.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow accountable self-implication and consequence, with the user’s consent about disclosure. An ending can complicate the opening rather than summarize it.
