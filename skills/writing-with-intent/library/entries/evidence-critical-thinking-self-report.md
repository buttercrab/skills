## When to use

Use when planning human review or writing about AI and cognition. Ask what the author will independently evaluate, especially where confidence in the tool exceeds their ability to check its output.

## What to inspect

The English survey included 319 weekly workplace GenAI users and 936 retained use examples. It measured reported critical-thinking enactment and perceived effort, not objective ability loss. Higher confidence in AI was associated with less reported critical engagement. The analysis describes verification, integration and oversight as important work that remains.

## What to borrow and adapt

Our application: make the human's review assignment concrete: verify the decisive fact, challenge the inference, or choose what the piece should mean. Let them mark uncertainty instead of treating acceptance as proof that checking occurred. For an article on cognitive effects, name the instrument and comparison before writing the headline.

## Mismatches and limits

Self-report can conflate reduced effort using a tool with reduced critical thinking, as the authors acknowledge. English fluency, regular use and a younger, technologically skilled sample limit transfer. Associations do not establish causation, long-term skill loss, brain damage or effects unique to writing. This card supplies no measured benefit for our proposed review prompts.
