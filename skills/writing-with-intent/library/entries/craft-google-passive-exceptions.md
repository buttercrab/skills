## When to use

The reader cannot tell who must act, or an editing pass mechanically rewrites every passive construction. Ask whether agency matters in this sentence and what the paragraph is currently about.

## What to inspect

Read the exceptions with the main preference. Google favors explicit actors for instructions but permits passive constructions for object emphasis, reduced emphasis on an actor, and situations where responsibility is irrelevant to the reader.

## What to borrow and adapt

In a procedure, specify that the operator approves a request if the reader must take that action. In an analysis following one dataset through several transformations, a passive sentence may preserve the dataset as the subject and avoid a distracting actor switch. For a failure report, however, omitting the responsible component can hide the mechanism. Compare the meanings and information order, not just verb labels.

## Mismatches and limits

These are Google documentation house choices. They do not establish a universal grammatical hierarchy or imply that passive writing is evasive. Unknown agency should remain unknown rather than be filled in for stylistic reasons. Avoid blaming a reader merely to satisfy an active-voice preference. This card is deliberately paired conceptually with Gopen and Swan's continuity analysis, not offered as another independent experiment.
