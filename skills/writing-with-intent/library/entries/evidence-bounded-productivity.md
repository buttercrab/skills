## When to use

Use when choosing where AI assistance may save effort. Ask which part is routine expression and which depends on private knowledge, exact facts or the writer's original judgment.

## What to inspect

This preregistered experiment randomized access before a second occupation-related writing task for 453 college-educated professionals. Experienced professionals graded outputs blind. The author manuscript reports approximately 40% less time and 18% higher output ratings with ChatGPT assistance. The brief prompts favored clear, persuasive, relatively generic writing. Headline estimates concern the variable-time incentive arms (roughly 80% of the sample), with the fixed-15-minute condition analyzed separately; access takeup and control use were imperfect.

## What to borrow and adapt

Our application: offer assistance on a bounded draft or revision while keeping a visible acceptance condition: what the reader should understand or do. Evaluate time saved separately from correctness and voice. Retain the user's material needed to judge whether generic wording fits the actual workplace.

## Mismatches and limits

The tasks did not require substantial organization-specific context or precise factual accuracy; short tasks do not establish long-form editorial value or durable skill gains. Sample and effects belong to the linked 453-person manuscript. The observational editing patterns do not prove that superficial editing is the best process.
