## When to use

When an argument turns on a word that readers use for several different things.

## What to inspect

The personal opening and subsequent exclusions of neighboring concepts.

## Craft trace

Question: what does this concept mean beyond social approval? A self-implicating episode motivates a definition by contrast; later examples test it.

## Revision implications

Our proposed transfer: ask what the writer means and what they explicitly exclude. Replace a general virtue claim with a consequential distinction the reader can examine.

## What to borrow and adapt

Borrow the definition task. Choose evidence and references this audience understands, in the user’s own language.

## Mismatches and limits

Literary examples are not empirical psychological evidence. Colonial imagery and dated generalizations need critique; do not reproduce them as timeless guidance or copy the voice.
