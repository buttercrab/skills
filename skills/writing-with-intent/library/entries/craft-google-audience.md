## When to use

A technical article is sprawling, assumes inconsistent background, or opens with history that does not help the intended reader. Ask what readers already understand, where they encounter the topic, and what they want to understand or accomplish. A job title alone is a weak audience definition.

## What to inspect

Read the audience distinctions alongside the document's explicit scope and prerequisite sections. The course ties document order to reader questions and supports comparison with concepts readers actually know.

## What to borrow and adapt

For an article about database migrations, distinguish an operator choosing a rollout from a student learning schema change. The first might need failure conditions early; the second may need a concrete before/after model first. Write a private scope sentence and compare every section against it. If the draft uncovers a better question, revise that sentence instead of automatically deleting the discovery.

## Mismatches and limits

This is a technical-document course, not a universal narrative formula. A memoir or exploratory essay can reward uncertainty and delayed resolution. Summaries at the start and explicit audience declarations are options, not mandatory published scaffolding. Google resources in this collection form one editorial family, not independent effectiveness studies.
