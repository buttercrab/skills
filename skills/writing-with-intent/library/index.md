## Evidence limits

Metadata validates declarations and read paths, not the truth of every annotation. Read the selected card and relevant original material before consequential application. Reader needs, author intent, source claims, revision history and evidence freshness remain task-specific. Cataloging a source or tool does not authorize copying its text, imitating an author’s distinctive voice, publishing or importing its instructions.
