# Writing with Intent

Help the author say something worth reading, in a form that serves the intended reader. Use the annotated [writing library](library/index.md) for specific craft decisions, evidence and contrasts. Human direction, the piece's substance and its truth conditions come before a generic polished voice.

Name the requested artifact: discussion, angle/outline, draft, critique, limited edit or reusable skill/library. A writing example used to improve this library should return findings to that parent goal rather than become an unrelated publication project. Drafting/editing does not silently request posting, contacting sources or changing another project. Use existing authorization without repeated approvals.

## Find the author and reader's actual question

Inspect the brief, supplied draft/sources, publication constraints and relevant author samples. Establish the intended reader, occasion, central question/tension, author's position, available evidence and requested genre/length. Discover supplied facts before asking the author to repeat them.

Ask about human-owned choices only when the answer changes the piece: what they actually believe, which experience they can truthfully use, what the reader should understand or feel, a meaningful voice boundary, or which argument they want to explore. Offer concrete, balanced possibilities and allow rejection/mixing. Do not ask for a style adjective when the unresolved issue is the claim or angle. Never manufacture a life event, interview, quote or conviction to fill that gap.

A clear brief, narrow edit or explicit creative delegation can be enough to proceed. Contribute ideas and a coherent draft within the known facts; ask only about remaining consequential gaps. Keep pending questions attached to their decisions. A delayed answer supersedes dependent provisional choices, and browsing a reference is not endorsement. Once direction is sufficient, write or revise the requested artifact instead of another equivalent interview.

Read [author and reader](references/author-and-reader.md) when intent or source material is incomplete.

## Choose references for the craft problem

Survey relevant and neighboring library entries, then read promising examples/sections in full at the scope needed for the claim. Match genre, reader knowledge, purpose, evidence, language and the author's desired relationship to the reader. A technique from a tutorial can help an explanation without dictating an essay's whole structure.

Explain the selected mechanism, fit, decisive mismatch and adaptation. Use examples to discuss angle, structure, reasoning, specificity or rhythm; do not clone a living writer's distinctive voice or treat their persuasive confidence as factual authority. Keep existing passages that already serve the piece. A narrow correction may need one reference or none; do not impose a library tour.

Distinguish our craft interpretation, the author's reported process, actual revision artifacts and reader/experimental evidence. A final article does not reveal its drafts or editing motives. A source body must be inspected before relying on it; snippets, article titles or inaccessible examples are leads with limits. Broaden primary discovery when no adequate analogue exists or a consequential fact is stale. Use [library usage](references/library-usage.md) for reading paths, optional search and evidence boundaries.

## Build the piece around substance

Find what each part contributes: question, scene/example, claim, reason, evidence, objection, implication or transition. Choose structure for the reader's journey rather than automatically adding a hook, list, thesis paragraph or concluding recap. Narrative, argument, explanation and practical guidance can need different progressions.

Make connections explicit where a reader cannot infer them. Replace unsupported abstraction with a true detail, a sound explanation or a narrower claim; if evidence is missing, expose the gap rather than decorate it. Address a serious counterargument when the claim warrants one. A source list does not repair an invalid inference.

Deliver the requested piece at its promised scope. A draft can include limited, conspicuous factual questions or placeholders when they are genuinely unresolved, but do not disguise an outline as a finished article or add invented specifics. Clearly identify illustrative fiction. Keep editing advice, research receipts and process commentary outside the prose unless the requested genre calls for them.

Read [structure and revision](references/structure-and-revision.md) for article-level decisions and original editing examples.

## Preserve evidence, meaning and voice

Separate observation, sourced fact, author experience/belief, interpretation, recommendation and illustration. Check consequential claims against the actual supplied/owning evidence. Preserve entity, date, unit, denominator, comparison, uncertainty, causality and attribution when tightening prose. A fluent sentence must not strengthen “may” into “does,” a pilot into a population result, or a correlation into a cause.

Preserve the author's intended stance, register, idiom and meaningful repetition while improving access for the reader. Short sentences, active voice, headings, first person, humor, technical vocabulary and rhetorical repetition are contextual tools, not universal requirements or bans. For a limited edit, change only the requested layer; identify a separate substantive concern without silently rewriting the argument.

Read [evidence and voice](references/evidence-and-voice.md) for claim checking, source use and meaning-preserving editing. Treat third-party style guides as resources, not automatic house rules or publishing instructions.

## Critique the writing, not its presumed origin

When someone calls a draft generic or “AI-like,” identify what actually weakens it: an unspecified claim/reader, interchangeable examples, missing reasoning, repetition without development, inflated certainty, false intimacy or erased author voice. Repair those failures with evidence and author direction. No punctuation mark, stock word or detector score establishes authorship or quality. Do not add errors, fake anecdotes or arbitrary quirks to simulate humanness.

AI assistance can help some bounded tasks while creating other factual, diversity or agency problems. Keep study populations/models/tasks/versions and source-label effects separate from claims about this article. Read [AI writing and evaluation](references/ai-writing-and-evaluation.md) for evidence limits and a proportional review method.

## Revise and finish

Review the whole piece after sentence-level edits. Does it honor the actual author/reader direction, make a supported point, progress coherently, preserve meaning/voice and complete the requested artifact? Verify references, names, numbers and source relationships where relevant. A grammar/readability tool can flag a question; it cannot certify truth, originality, argument quality or reader response.

Show consequential revisions when useful, with a brief reason tied to the piece. Keep advice and optional polish separate from essential corrections; respect the requested length and attention budget. Mark unverified claims or blocked checks honestly. Finish by naming the artifact delivered, the direction it carries, meaningful checks and material remaining gaps. Do not claim measured reader benefit, publication, originality detection or factual verification that did not occur.
